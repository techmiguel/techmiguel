<h1 align="left">Hello</h1>

###

<p align="left">I'm Miguel Angel, an Automation Engineer (CUJAE, Havana). I help teams take connected devices from idea to working product: firmware, electronics, enclosure and on-device AI.</p>

###

<h2 align="left">How I can help</h2>

###

<ul>
  <li><b>Firmware</b> in C/C++ for ESP32, ESP8266 and 8051-family microcontrollers, with Wi-Fi, Ethernet and MQTT connectivity and integration with Home Assistant and ESPHome.</li>
  <li><b>On-device AI</b>: TinyML models trained, optimized and deployed on microcontrollers, so your product responds with low latency and low power, without depending on the cloud.</li>
  <li><b>Battery-powered devices</b>: lithium-ion cells with safe charging and protection, and firmware designed around the power budget to extend runtime.</li>
  <li><b>Hardware design</b> in KiCad, from schematic to boards ready for manufacturing and full assembly, and <b>3D enclosure design</b> in FreeCAD.</li>
  <li><b>Complete deliverables</b>: every project measured, tested and documented in English, so your team can maintain it and build on it.</li>
</ul>

###

<h2 align="left">Featured projects</h2>

###

<ul>
  <li><a href="https://github.com/techmiguel/neo-robot"><b>NEO</b></a>: voice assistant built on an ESP32-S3. A 15 KB int8 TensorFlow Lite Micro model detects the wake word on the device, and a Python server handles speech recognition, intent detection and speech synthesis.</li>
  <li><a href="https://github.com/techmiguel/smartRele"><b>SmartRele</b></a>: open-hardware Wi-Fi relay module for 110–120 VAC and loads up to 10 A, based on the ESP-12F. Designed in KiCad with zero DRC/ERC errors and ready for fully assembled manufacturing.</li>
</ul>
